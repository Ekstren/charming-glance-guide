## Ravager

- **Dungeons / Mirage:** Night Curse, Solaris Storm, Soul Piercer, Flickering Blade; Piercing Assault, Tactical Adaptation, Blade of Lament, Indomitable Will. For a safer run, keep Indomitable Will; otherwise test Soulfire Protection or Insightful Eye.
- **Crucible / Conquest:** Flame Aura, Solaris Storm, Eclipse Slash, Flickering Blade; Piercing Assault, Tactical Adaptation, Blade of Judgment, Insightful Eye. Replace Flame Aura with Sunset Sword if its higher rarity performs better; replace Insightful Eye with Crit Mastery when Crit is covered.
- **Arena / 4v4 Tournament:** Shattering Dance, Sunset Sword, Flickering Blade, Blade Storm; Piercing Assault, Tactical Adaptation, Soulfire Protection, Indomitable Will. The source supplies a PvP build, not separate Arena and 4v4 bars.

## Templar

- **Dungeons / 4v4 Tournament, tank:** Valor Surge, Heart of Challenge, Lunarwater Threads, Hamper Strike; Iron Will, Block Mastery, Block Awareness, Soul Protection. Drop Block Awareness once Block is sufficient, then flex to Rebound or Oath of Vigil.
- **Mirage, damage:** Swirling Blade, Lunarwater Threads, Seismic Tide, Raging Maelstrom; Frigid Aura, Defensive Assault, Frigid Glint, Sacred Rhythm. For AoE, Sacred Rhythm can become Blade of Lament or Pursuit of Victory.
- **Crucible / Conquest:** No separate T5 boss or score bar is published. Start from the Water build for personal damage, or the generic defensive build when supporting a carry; treat this mapping as provisional.
- **Arena / PvP:** Valor Surge, Swirling Blade, Star Shattering Slash, Iron Slashes; Defensive Assault, Block Mastery, Block Awareness, Soul Protection. If Block is sufficient, flex Block Awareness to Sacred Rhythm or Physical Mastery. The published guide does not give a distinct 4v4 recipe.

## Magister

- **Dungeons / Mirage:** Formation Breaker, Wind Blade Spiral, Storm Rhapsody, Howling Hurricane; Radiant Warp, Thunder Judgment, Cyclone Lament, Radiant Sear.
- **Crucible / Conquest:** Formation Breaker, Divine Wrath, Twin Gale, Thunder of Judgment; Radiant Warp, Thunderbolt Mark, Mana Surge, Radiant Sear. Against high Wind resistance or a stationary boss, test Meteoric Flames; Cyclone Lament can replace Thunderbolt Mark with a Wind-heavy bar.
- **Arena / 4v4 Tournament:** Formation Breaker, Wind Blade Spiral, Twin Gale, Thunder of Judgment; Radiant Warp, Thunder Judgment, Vital Rhythm, Cyclone Lament. The guide provides one PvP bar for both; keep Radiant Warp for mobility and survival.
- **Dungeon horde alternative:** Formation Breaker, Fiery Star Trail, Crimson Whirl, Meteoric Flames; Radiant Warp, Ember Flare, Explosive Spirit, Fiery Burst.

## Prophet

- **Dungeons / Mirage, damage:** Thalasson Summon, Abyssal Hand, Desperate Shadow, Hexed Blast; Soul Pact Resonance, Night's Blessing, Shadowy Current, Mana Surge. The summon is fragile, so swap it if it dies too quickly.
- **Crucible / Conquest, direct damage:** Thalasson Summon, Desperate Shadow, Hexed Blast, Soul Reap; Shadowy Current, Shadow Vengeance, Mana Surge, Soul Pact Resonance. Use Frenzy Totem instead of Thalasson Summon if the summon cannot survive. For long fights, compare the Pure Erosion bar; the guide says its value depends on survival, buffs, and account stats.
- **Arena / PvP:** Use the direct single-target setup, normally replacing Thalasson Summon with Frenzy Totem. Replace Mana Surge with Ring of Omen against targets with high Effect Resistance.
- **Healing role:** Rejuvenating Rain, Radiant Rhythm, Radiant Restoration, Waterling Summon; Phantom Light, Healing Mastery, Overhealing, plus a flex Charm. Use Resurrection when allies are dying, Shadow Vengeance when you are dying, or Mantra of Blessings when the team is safe.

Before activating T5, recheck these pages and confirm the live tier. The guides can change, and some activity entries above are mappings from a shared class build rather than separately published meta builds.
